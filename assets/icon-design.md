A single bold stylized pulse/forward mark formed by two smooth connected chevrons, suggesting fast token flow. Geometric, balanced, restrained, modern desktop utility aesthetic. Symbol thick, clean, no tiny details, no text, no letters, no axes, no charts, no bars, no gradients, no 3D, no shadows, no glow, no outlines, no additional ornaments. Large simple negative spaces.
